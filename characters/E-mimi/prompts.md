```text

Asset type: full-body character turnaround reference sheet for future clothing try-on.
Primary request: A high-resolution wide photographic sheet with four equal panels of the SAME adult woman referenced in these five photos: true front, 45-degree three-quarter, true 90-degree side and straight back views. Image 2 shows the target adult woman's clear side facial profile and long wavy hair, and is the primary profile and standard adult appearance reference. In image 3 the target is ONLY the woman in the center wearing a headband; use her visible eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips and natural face proportions, ignoring other people, wet clothes, headband and intense expression. Image 4 supplies another side facial contour, ignore the items she carries, coat and store. Image 5 supplies the front smiling face of the target woman in black, ignore the room and watermark. Use the shared facial geometry of the target subject across the original references; do not include other subjects. Standardize hairstyle and expression as specified below.
Subject: character E / Mimi, adult woman. User-supplied height 166 cm, D-cup wardrobe sizing, and long slender legs. Her legs should be slim and elegant with a plausible adult torso-to-leg ratio, natural knee and calf volume, not artificially stretched or skeletal. The originals do not clearly show her full unclothed leg shape or whole body; waist, hips, legs and back are conservative provisional completions following user settings, not measured facts.
Face: small oval-to-heart facial outline with softly narrowing jaw and rounded tapered chin. Expressive fairly large dark almond-shaped eyes with their individual source spacing, clearly defined upper eyelid contour, natural gently arched dark brows, a well-defined relatively straight nose with the specific projection and tip silhouette seen in images 2 and 4, soft rose-toned lips with visible cupid's bow and slightly fuller lower lip. Use the same part, shade, length and waves in all panels. Let it fall naturally around lower chest to mid-back. Keep eyes and jaw silhouette visible.
Body and outfit: naturally slender adult stature at the user's 166 cm setting, slim shoulder line and arms, natural full bust consistent with D-cup as user wardrobe context, comfortable defined waist and moderate hips. No bust enhancement or exaggerated hourglass silhouette. Identical fully opaque plain cream fitted short-sleeve crew-neck knit top and matte charcoal-gray slim straight-leg full-length trousers in every view; trousers should show the user's slender-leg shape with realistic comfortable fabric fit rather than loose wide-leg fabric. Gentle relaxed closed-mouth smile, same expression across views. No crossed legs, hip tilts, runway posing or lengthening fashion perspective. Head and torso turn together across views. Realistic skin pores, natural light neutral-warm complexion and knit texture, restrained natural makeup, no heavy smoothing.

Constraints: preserve the specific original adult facial likeness, same adult age appearance, proportions, hair, clothing and lighting across all views. Respect user slim-leg setting without excessive elongation. No background people, screenshot elements, logos, watermark, headbands, accessories, cartoon, 3D render, excessive glamour retouching, exaggerated curves, childlike appearance, cropped feet or anatomical errors.
```

Constraints: same person, same adult age appearance, facial geometry, hairstyle, expression, clothes and lighting across all panels. Preserve the source profile rather than inventing a generic nose. No other people, headbands, watermark, usernames, logos, artificial symmetry, overly large eyes, pointed chin, changed lip shape, childlike styling, cartoon, 3D render, cropped hair crown or anatomical distortions.
